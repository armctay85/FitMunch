# FitMunch logo: 5g, FM with a bite

## Folders
- `svg/`: vector masters. The wordmark is converted to outlines, so no font is needed.
- `png/`: transparent PNGs: mark 1024 px, horizontal lockup 400 px tall, stacked lockup 1000 px wide.
- `icons/`: favicon.ico (16/32/48), favicon.svg, favicon-16/32/48.png, apple-touch-icon.png (180), icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-512-maskable.png.

## Colour versions
| Version | Mark | Wordmark | Use on |
|---|---|---|---|
| full-colour | Mint | Off-white | Forest (default) |
| reversed | Off-white | Off-white | Forest, or beside another brand's colour |
| one-colour | Forest | Forest | White and light grounds |
| black | Black | Black | Print, single-colour |

Mint is never used on white.

## Colours
- Forest `#0F2A1F` (RGB 15 42 31)
- Mint `#6EF2B0` (RGB 110 242 176)
- Off-white `#F3F0E8` (RGB 243 240 232)

## Sizes
- Full mark from 32 px and up.
- Below 32 px use `fitmunch-mark-small-*`: the same trolley, bite, handle and wheels, without the FM.
- Horizontal lockup no narrower than 120 px.

## Clear space
Keep one wheel's diameter clear on every side of the mark and both lockups, including next to a trainer's logo.

## Wordmark
Archivo ExtraBold (800), tracking -4.5%, one colour, "FitMunch" with a capital F and M.
Horizontal lockup: the wordmark is 0.74 x the mark's height, with a gap of 0.2 x. Stacked lockup: the wordmark is 0.4 x the mark's height.
