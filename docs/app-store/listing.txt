DESCRIPTION
PASTE
FitMunch is the daily iPhone app for meals, coaching, workouts, a receipt scan, and your history.

Today
Home shows calories, protein, carbs, and fats for the day you are viewing. Meals you have logged sit on that screen, with what is still left against your targets.

Log a meal
The plus button logs a meal on the device. You name the meal and add foods, then the totals update. A free account can log 3 meals for the day you are viewing.

Meal plan
On Meals, set a calorie target and a protein target, choose a goal, and generate a 7-day plan. The goal picker includes general fitness, fat loss, muscle gain, and maintain. Those are labels on the plan, not a promise of a result. Each day lists breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a snack, with calories, protein, and prep time. The screen is written for a Woolworths or Coles week.

Coach
The AI coach is a chat for food and training. You can ask what to eat after training. Replies come from your FitMunch account.

Workout
Generate a weekly plan for the gym or at home, at beginner, intermediate, or advanced. Tick a day when you finish it. Log an exercise with sets, reps, and weight in kilograms. The steps figure is a counter in the app: tap to add 1,000 toward the goal you set.

Scan a shop
Photograph a grocery receipt, or choose one from your photo library. FitMunch reads a Woolworths, Coles, Aldi, or IGA receipt for each item's macros and a haul score. You can log that haul to today's meals.

History
History lists the meals you logged, with averages and a calorie chart. Ranges are a week, a month, three months, or a custom range. Premium can export that history.

Premium
FitMunch Premium is an auto-renewing subscription.
Monthly: fitmunch_monthly
Annual: fitmunch_annual
The price you pay is the price shown on the paywall before you confirm. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings after purchase. Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings.

Premium removes the 3-meal cap for the day you are viewing, and it opens full history and export.

Your data
Meals and workout logs you enter are stored on the device. The coach, meal plans, and receipt scans use your FitMunch account, so those actions need a connection.

DO NOT CLAIM
These are true of build 9 and must stay out of the public text.

The two subscriptions have no introductory offer in FitMunchProducts.storekit. The create-account line about a 14-day Premium trial is account-screen copy. It is not the App Store offer, so this listing does not mention a trial.

Info.plist contains Health usage strings. No Swift code calls HealthKit. Steps are the in-app +1,000 control. There is no Apple Watch target.

Continue on the web opens fitmunch.com.au in Safari. There is no Link payment in the app.
