READINESS CHECKLIST

Identity
[ ] The uploaded binary is marketing version 1.0 and build 9 (project.yml MARKETING_VERSION "1.0", CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION "9").
[ ] The bundle ID is com.fitmunch.app.
[ ] The app is iPhone-only (TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY 1). On iPad it runs in compatibility mode.

Subscriptions
[ ] scripts/check-iap-product-ids.mjs passes. Sellable product IDs are exactly fitmunch_monthly and fitmunch_annual.
[ ] fitmunch_weekly is not in FitMunch Swift sources or the StoreKit file.
[ ] FitMunchProducts.storekit lists those two subscriptions only. Monthly display price in that file is 19.99, period P1M. Annual display price is 149.99, period P1Y. introductoryOffer is null on both.
[ ] The paywall loads prices from the App Store for the reviewer's storefront. Do not tell the reviewer a single hard-coded charge. The in-repo catalog prices above are the reference prices in the StoreKit file.
[ ] The create-account screen shows: Free to start · 14-day Premium trial, no card needed. That sentence is account-screen copy. It is not an introductory offer on the two subscriptions. The StoreKit file does not attach an introductory offer.
[ ] The last onboarding step shows a Premium card labeled $9.99 per month. Tapping it only opens the paywall. It is not the App Store price and it does not purchase. The prices to review are the ones on the paywall.
[ ] A Sandbox Apple ID can buy the selected plan with Subscribe Now, or the paywall shows the load-failure copy and Retry. The screen must not be blank.

Where the reviewer goes
[ ] There is no guest mode and no built-in demo login. The reviewer creates a free account on the first screen (Create Account). Password must be at least 8 characters. That calls POST https://www.fitmunch.com.au/api/auth/register. A network connection is required.
[ ] After that, onboarding asks for a name and email again, then Get Started. The tabs are Today, Plan, Scan, Coach, and Me. Plan has Meals and Training. Progress is the first row on Me. There is no More tab.
[ ] Me, Upgrade and Me, Upgrade to Premium both open the paywall full screen.
[ ] Restore Purchases is on the paywall (under the plans, and again next to Retry if plans fail to load) and on Me, under Subscription.
[ ] While plans are loading, the paywall shows Loading plans. If both fetches come back empty, it shows: Plans couldn't load. Check your connection and try again. Retry reloads. Pull to refresh reloads.
[ ] Scan, Choose from library works with no camera. Take a photo with no camera, or with camera access off, shows an alert titled Camera not available with Choose from library.
[ ] The app does not use the microphone. Info.plist and project.yml have no NSMicrophoneUsageDescription. The camera session takes a still photo and sets automaticallyConfiguresApplicationAudioSession to false.

Do not claim these are live
[ ] HealthKit is not used. Info.plist contains NSHealthShareUsageDescription and NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription, but no Swift file imports or calls HealthKit. Workout steps are an in-app counter (+1,000). They are not Apple Health.
[ ] There is no Apple Watch target and no Watch code.
[ ] There is no Link payment in the app. Continue on the web opens https://www.fitmunch.com.au in Safari. That website checkout is separate from the two App Store subscriptions. Review purchases with Subscribe Now and a Sandbox Apple ID.
[ ] The archive workflow uploads a build. It does not submit for review. Do not treat a green CI run as App Review approval.

Account removal the reviewer can use
[ ] Me, Delete Account, Delete Everything calls DELETE /auth/account and removes the server account.
[ ] Me, Log Out signs out. Local meal logs stay on the device.
[ ] Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are linked from the paywall and from Me (https://fitmunch.com.au/privacy and https://fitmunch.com.au/terms). Contact Support opens mail to support@fitmunch.com.au.

(App Store Connect review notes for this version. Stay under 4000 characters. No demo password is built into the app.)

FitMunch 1.0 (9), bundle ID com.fitmunch.app. No existing account is required.

Create an account. The first screen opens on Create Account. Enter a name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters, then tap Create Free Account. This calls the same register service as fitmunch.com.au. A network connection is required. No card is requested. The line under the button reads: Free to start · 14-day Premium trial, no card needed. That line is account-screen copy. It is not an introductory offer on the subscriptions.

Onboarding. Tap Next. Enter a name and email (the goal can stay on Maintenance). Tap Next, then Get Started. The Premium card labeled $9.99 per month only opens the paywall. It does not purchase, and $9.99 is not the App Store price.

Tabs. Today, Plan, Scan, Coach, and Me are on the tab bar. There is no More tab. Plan switches between Meals and Training. Progress is the first row on Me. On iPad this is the iPhone app in compatibility mode.

Today: the plus button logs a meal on the device. A free account can log 3 meals for the day you are viewing. The next tap shows Free limit reached and opens the paywall.
Coach: send a question. The reply comes from the FitMunch account service. If it asks the account to upgrade, the paywall opens.
Scan: you do not need a camera. Tap Choose from library and pick a grocery receipt photo. The app sends it for a haul score when the service responds. Take a photo with no camera, or with camera access off, shows an alert titled Camera not available, with Choose from library. The camera takes a still photo only. This build does not use the microphone and does not ask for microphone permission.
Plan, Meals: goal labels are General, Cut, Build, and Maintain. Tap Generate 7-day plan. If the service asks for an upgrade, the paywall opens.
Plan, Training: the weekly plan is generated on the device. Daily steps are an in-app counter (tap +1,000). They are not Apple Health. Log an exercise on the device. This build does not call HealthKit and has no Apple Watch app. Info.plist contains Health usage strings, but no HealthKit API is used.
Progress: open Me, then Progress. A free account shows an alert, Upgrade to premium to access full history.

Subscriptions. Two auto-renewable products. No weekly plan.
Monthly: fitmunch_monthly (fallback title Monthly Premium).
Annual: fitmunch_annual (fallback title Annual Premium). The annual card can show a Save 20% label. The charge is the App Store price on the card.
The paywall shows the App Store price for the reviewer's storefront. The project StoreKit file lists reference prices of 19.99 per month and 149.99 per year, and introductoryOffer is null on both products.
Open the paywall from Me, Upgrade, or Me, Upgrade to Premium. It also opens from Today after the third meal, from Coach or Plan when an upgrade is required, and from the onboarding Premium card. Select a plan and tap Subscribe Now with a Sandbox Apple ID.

Loading plans. The paywall shows Loading plans while it fetches fitmunch_monthly and fitmunch_annual. It tries once more on its own. If both tries are empty it stays on the paywall and shows: Plans couldn't load. Check your connection and try again. Tap Retry. Pull to refresh does the same. The screen is not left blank.

Restore Purchases. On the paywall, under the plans. The same button sits next to Retry if plans fail to load. It is also on Me, under Subscription, Restore Purchases. Restore looks for fitmunch_monthly and fitmunch_annual and then shows an alert.

Not in this build: Apple Watch, HealthKit, microphone, and Link pay. Continue on the web opens the FitMunch website in Safari. That is separate from these subscriptions. Please review purchases with Subscribe Now and a Sandbox Apple ID.

Account. Me, Log Out keeps on-device meals. Me, Delete Account, Delete Everything deletes the server account.
Privacy: https://fitmunch.com.au/privacy
Terms: https://fitmunch.com.au/terms
Support: support@fitmunch.com.au (Me, Contact Support).
