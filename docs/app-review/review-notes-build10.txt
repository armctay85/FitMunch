FitMunch App Review packet
iOS version 1.0, build 10, bundle ID com.fitmunch.app

This file is notes for the person submitting build 10. It does not submit the app.
Paste only the block marked PASTE INTO APP REVIEW NOTES into App Store Connect.

READINESS CHECKLIST

Identity
[ ] The uploaded binary is marketing version 1.0 and build 10 (project.yml MARKETING_VERSION "1.0", CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION "10").
[ ] The bundle ID is com.fitmunch.app.
[ ] The app is iPhone-only (TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY 1). On iPad it runs in compatibility mode.
[ ] project.yml info.properties includes UILaunchScreen (LaunchBackground, LaunchLogo), portrait only, UIRequiresFullScreen, and ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption false. The public RevenueCat SDK key is in that same block so XcodeGen keeps it in the binary.

Subscriptions
[ ] scripts/check-iap-product-ids.mjs passes. Sellable product IDs are exactly fitmunch_monthly and fitmunch_annual.
[ ] fitmunch_weekly is not in FitMunch Swift sources or the StoreKit file.
[ ] FitMunchProducts.storekit lists those two subscriptions only. Monthly display price in that file is 19.99, period P1M. Annual display price is 149.99, period P1Y. Each has a free introductory offer of 14 days (P14D). Purchase dialogs are enabled on that configuration.
[ ] The paywall loads prices from the App Store for the reviewer's storefront. The button reads Subscribe for {displayPrice}/{period} unless that product has an introductory free offer and the Sandbox account is eligible. Eligible trial copy is Start {period} free trial, then {displayPrice}/{period}, cancel anytime. The annual card shows Best value, a per-week price (annual divided by 52), and the percent saved versus 12 times the monthly price. None of those strings are hard-coded dollar amounts in the view.
[ ] The create-account screen shows: Free to start. Premium trial available in-app. That sentence is account-screen copy. The StoreKit file does not attach an introductory offer.
[ ] The last onboarding step shows a Premium card. The price is the monthly product displayPrice from StoreKit when it loads, otherwise the card says See plans. Tapping it only opens the paywall. It does not purchase.
[ ] A Sandbox Apple ID can buy the selected plan with the green button, or the paywall shows: Plans couldn't load. Check your connection and try again. plus Retry and Restore. There is no configuration card and no website checkout button.

Where the reviewer goes
[ ] There is no guest mode and no built-in demo login. The reviewer creates a free account on the first screen (Create Account). Password must be at least 8 characters. That calls POST https://www.fitmunch.com.au/api/auth/register. A network connection is required.
[ ] After that, onboarding asks for a name and email again, then Get Started. The tabs are Today, Plan, Scan, Coach, and Me. Plan has Meals and Training. Progress is the first row on Me. There is no More tab.
[ ] Me, Upgrade and Me, Upgrade to Premium both open the paywall full screen.
[ ] Restore is on the paywall (footnote, and again next to Retry if plans fail to load) and on Me, under Subscription.
[ ] While plans are loading, the paywall shows Loading plans. If both fetches come back empty, it shows: Plans couldn't load. Check your connection and try again. Retry reloads. Pull to refresh reloads.
[ ] The paywall headline is Eat to your goals with every shop. Benefit rows are unlimited receipt scans and haul scores, an AI coach that knows your shop, a high-protein week planned for you, and unlimited meal logging and full history. Annual is preselected.
[ ] Scan, Choose from library works with no camera. Take a photo with no camera, or with camera access off, shows an alert titled Camera not available with Choose from library.
[ ] The app does not use the microphone. Info.plist and project.yml have no NSMicrophoneUsageDescription. The camera session takes a still photo and sets automaticallyConfiguresApplicationAudioSession to false.

Do not claim these are live
[ ] This build does not read or write Apple Health. Info.plist has no NSHealthShareUsageDescription and no NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription. Workout has no daily steps counter.
[ ] There is no Apple Watch target and no Watch code.
[ ] Purchases are the two auto-renewable subscriptions on the paywall. There is no website checkout button in the iOS target.
[ ] The archive workflow uploads a build. It does not submit for review. Do not treat a green CI run as App Review approval.

App Store privacy nutrition label (match PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy)
Data is collected. Data is not used to track the user. No tracking domains.
Name: linked to the user, not used for tracking, App Functionality.
Email Address: linked to the user, not used for tracking, App Functionality.
Health: linked to the user, not used for tracking, App Functionality and Product Personalization. This is meals and macros saved to the account. It is not HealthKit.
Photos or Videos: linked to the user, not used for tracking, App Functionality. Receipt photos are uploaded while signed in.
User ID: linked to the user, not used for tracking, App Functionality and Analytics. The account id is sent to RevenueCat with logIn.
Purchase History: linked to the user, not used for tracking, App Functionality and Analytics.
Product Interaction: linked to the user, not used for tracking, App Functionality. Feature counts for the free AI cap are stored on the account.
Other User Content: linked to the user, not used for tracking, App Functionality. Coach messages are the user's free text, posted to the account service and forwarded to the model provider.
Other Data Types: linked to the user, not used for tracking, App Functionality. This is age on the account, sent with Coach. Weight and height stay under Health.
Do not select Fitness. Workout logs stay on the device. The iOS app does not call /workouts/log. Do not select Payment Info, Device ID, Crash Data, Performance Data, or advertising data.
AI consent (Guideline 5.1.2(i)): one choice, stored on the account. The first Coach send, the first receipt scan, and the first meal-plan generate show the same sheet. Buttons are Allow and Not now. Not now leaves those three off and the rest of the app usable. Me, Privacy can turn it off later. Coach sends up to 12 recent messages, including replies. The server keeps at most 20. Age, weight, height, goal, and diet come from the account profile. Providers are Google (Gemini), then xAI (Grok), then OpenAI, then Anthropic. A receipt photo goes to Google (Gemini) only. A failed read can return a built-in sample list, which the app does not show as the shop. A meal plan sends the goal as a label, calories, protein, and the number of days on that same chain.

Account removal the reviewer can use
[ ] Me, Delete Account, Delete Everything calls DELETE /auth/account and removes the server account.
[ ] Me, Log Out signs out. Local meal logs stay on the device.
[ ] Terms and Privacy are linked from the paywall footnote and from Me (https://fitmunch.com.au/terms and https://fitmunch.com.au/privacy). Contact Support opens mail to support@fitmunch.com.au.


PASTE INTO APP REVIEW NOTES
(App Store Connect review notes for this version. Stay under 4000 characters. No demo password is built into the app.)

FitMunch 1.0 (10), bundle ID com.fitmunch.app. No existing account is required.

Create an account. The first screen opens on Create Account. Enter a name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters, then tap Create Free Account. This calls the same register service as fitmunch.com.au. A network connection is required. No card is requested. The line under the button reads: Free to start. Premium trial available in-app. That line is account-screen copy. The project StoreKit file does not attach an introductory offer.

Onboarding. Tap Next. Enter a name and email (the goal can stay on Maintenance). Tap Next, then Get Started. The Premium card opens the paywall. It does not purchase. Its price is the App Store monthly price when StoreKit returns one.

Tabs. Today, Plan, Scan, Coach, and Me are on the tab bar. There is no More tab. Plan switches between Meals and Training. Progress is the first row on Me. On iPad this is the iPhone app in compatibility mode.

Today: the plus button logs a meal on the device. A free account can log 3 meals for the day you are viewing. The next tap shows Free limit reached and opens the paywall.
Coach: one sheet before the first send, scan, or meal plan. Buttons are Allow and Not now. Not now leaves those three off and the rest of the app usable. Me, Privacy can turn it off later. An upgrade opens the paywall.
Scan: you do not need a camera. Tap Choose from library and pick a grocery receipt photo. The app sends it for a haul score when the service responds. Take a photo with no camera, or with camera access off, shows an alert titled Camera not available, with Choose from library. The camera takes a still photo only. This build does not use the microphone and does not ask for microphone permission.
Plan, Meals: tap Generate 7-day plan. If the service asks for an upgrade, the paywall opens.
Plan, Training: the weekly plan is generated on the device. Log an exercise on the device. This build has no daily steps counter, does not call HealthKit, and has no Apple Watch app.
Progress: open Me, then Progress. A free account shows an alert, Upgrade to premium to access full history.

Subscriptions. Two auto-renewable products. No weekly plan.
Monthly: fitmunch_monthly (fallback title Monthly Premium).
Annual: fitmunch_annual (fallback title Annual Premium). Annual is preselected and can show Best value, a per-week price, and a savings percent. Both numbers are computed from the App Store prices (annual divided by 52, and the percent saved versus 12 times monthly).
The paywall shows the App Store price for the reviewer's storefront. The FitMunchStoreKit scheme uses FitMunchProducts.storekit: 19.99 per month and 149.99 per year, each with a 14-day free introductory offer. App Review uses the products in App Store Connect. The button reads Start {length} free trial, then {price}/{period}, cancel anytime, when that product has a free intro and the Sandbox account is eligible. Otherwise it reads Subscribe for {price}/{period}.
Open the paywall from Me, Upgrade, or Me, Upgrade to Premium. It also opens from Today after the third meal, from Coach or Plan when an upgrade is required, and from the onboarding Premium card. Select a plan and tap the green button with a Sandbox Apple ID.

Loading plans. The paywall shows Loading plans while it fetches fitmunch_monthly and fitmunch_annual. It tries once more on its own. If both tries are empty it stays on the paywall and shows: Plans couldn't load. Check your connection and try again. Tap Retry.

Restore. On the paywall footnote. The same action sits next to Retry if plans fail to load. It is also on Me, under Subscription, Restore Purchases. Restore looks for fitmunch_monthly and fitmunch_annual and then shows an alert.

Account. Me, Log Out keeps on-device meals. Me, Delete Account, Delete Everything deletes the server account.
