How to meal prep for the week on a budget

Pick 2 proteins, 2 carbs and 3 vegetables and use them across 10 meals. Cook the proteins and carbs on Sunday, keep sauces separate, and leave 1 fresh night. A high protein prep week works the same way: build each meal around chicken, mince, eggs, tuna or lentils first, then fill the plate. FitMunch writes the week this way and flags where a special makes a swap worthwhile.

Questions people ask

How do I make a meal plan and grocery list?
Choose the meals first, then total every ingredient across the week and group it by aisle. FitMunch does both steps: commit a week and it writes the list for you.
What should I buy for meal prep?
Start with proteins that reheat well, like chicken thigh or breast, beef mince, eggs and tinned tuna. Add bulk carbs such as rice, oats and sweet potato, then sturdy vegetables like broccoli, carrots and frozen mixed veg.
How do I meal prep for the week high protein?
Set a protein target first, then anchor every meal to a protein source before adding carbs and veg. FitMunch writes the week from the target you log.
Is there a printable weekly meal plan and grocery list template?
A blank template still leaves the maths to you. FitMunch writes the filled-in list from your week, and you can print it or copy it straight from the browser.
How much is a weekly shop in Australia?
It depends on who you feed and what is on special that week, so we will not quote an average. The FitMunch draft shows the total for your list from public catalogue specials before you leave home.
