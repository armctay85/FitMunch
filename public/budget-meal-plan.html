Questions people ask

Who is the plan written for?
You. FitMunch writes a plan from your own budget and targets, and you shop the list yourself.
Will the plan stay under my budget?
FitMunch uses your weekly budget when it writes the plan. It does not see live supermarket prices, so what you pay at the checkout can differ.
Can I use it for dietary needs?
Yes. Add dietary requirements such as vegetarian and the plan is written around them.
Does FitMunch order the food for me?
No. FitMunch writes the list and you check out yourself. FitMunch never places or pays for your supermarket order.
