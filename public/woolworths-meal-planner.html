Questions people ask

Is there a Woolworths meal planner app?
Woolworths publishes meal plans and recipes with shopping lists on its own site. FitMunch is a separate web app that runs in your browser. It writes a full 7-day plan from your body targets and drafts a Woolworths trolley. FitMunch is not affiliated with Woolworths. Check the shelf price at the store.
Is there an AI meal planner for Woolworths?
FitMunch plans the week from the targets you log, then drafts the trolley. It is not a live trolley feed. Check the shelf price at the store.
Can I get my Woolworths meal plan as a PDF?
You can copy the list or print it straight from the browser, then take it into the store or type it into your own Woolworths online order.
How do I plan a week of meals for 1, 2 or 4?
Pick meals that share staples, cook in batches and repeat lunches. FitMunch writes the ingredient list from the meals you commit, so the quantities follow the week you lock rather than a fixed family size.

No. FitMunch drafts the trolley. You check out at Woolworths yourself and pay the store directly.
