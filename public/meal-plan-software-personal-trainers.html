Questions trainers ask

What does meal plan software for personal trainers do?
FitMunch Coach writes a 7-day meal plan from the targets you set for that client, then drafts a shopping list. You share it with a PT-branded link or a PDF.
How much is FitMunch Coach?
A$39 a month for up to 10 clients, or A$79 a month for unlimited clients. Both include a 14-day trial. A card is required. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged.
Does my client pay for FitMunch Coach?
You pay the Coach subscription. The client receives the week through the share link or PDF.
Does FitMunch order or pay at the supermarket?
No. The list is an ingredient draft. The client checks out at Woolworths, Coles or Aldi and pays the store.
Are these meal plans medical advice?
No. FitMunch is food planning support, not medical advice. Clients should see a dietitian for medical nutrition.
Is FitMunch affiliated with Woolworths, Coles or Aldi?
No. Those names are trade marks of their owners. FitMunch does not use a live store account.
