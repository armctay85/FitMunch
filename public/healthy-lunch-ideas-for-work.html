How it works

A work lunch is one meal in the same week as breakfast and dinner. FitMunch writes all of them to the targets you set, then one shopping list you can pack from.

01

Put lunch on the week

Commit lunches with the other meals. They share the same calorie and protein targets.

02

One list

Ingredients for lunch and dinner are added up together. You shop once for the week you locked.

03

Pack from the list

You check out at the store, then pack lunch from what you bought. FitMunch does not place the order.

Questions people ask

Are these lunches a health claim?
No. They are meals in the weekly plan. FitMunch does not promise a health result.
Can lunch share foods with dinner?
Yes. Plan them in the same week and shared foods become one line on the shopping list.
Does this suit one person?
Yes. A week can be sized to you. A household week is a different page if more people eat the same dinners.
