Getting started

FitMunch helps you track nutrition, scan receipts, plan meals, and stay on top of your food goals. New to FitMunch? Start with the homepage or check pricing.

Upload a photo of your supermarket receipt in the browser. FitMunch extracts items and matches nutrition data. The photo is processed and not stored on our servers. Saved meals and plans stay with your account.

Common questions

How do I cancel or manage an App Store subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your Apple ID, then Subscriptions. Select FitMunch to change or cancel. In the FitMunch app you can also open Settings, Subscription, Manage Subscription. That opens the same Apple page. Cancel before the period ends and the next renewal does not run. Access stays until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I cancel or manage a web subscription?

A plan bought on fitmunch.com.au uses the billing portal, not the App Store. Open the Manage or cancel link in your trial email, or sign in and choose Manage subscription. The billing portal is where you update the card or cancel. Access stays until the end of the paid period.

How do I restore purchases?

In the FitMunch app, open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. The same button is in Settings, Subscription, Restore Purchases. Use the Apple ID that bought the subscription. Restore looks up that App Store purchase and applies it on this device.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, open Settings, then Delete Account, then Delete Everything. That permanently deletes your FitMunch account and the data stored with it, including meals, plans, and progress. Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel that under Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions if you do not want it to renew. A web subscription is cancelled with the account when billing can be reached. If deletion fails, email support@fitmunch.com.au.
