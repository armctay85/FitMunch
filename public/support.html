Billing

How do I manage my subscription?

Sign in and open Billing. Billing opens the Stripe customer portal, where you update the card, see invoices, and cancel. Use Manage subscription on this page. That link signs you in, then Billing calls the existing portal route.

How are payments taken?

All payments are processed securely via Stripe. Paid plans use a 14-day trial with a card on file. You can cancel at any time in Billing. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged.

Receipts or invoices

For a receipt or an invoice, see the refund policy or email support@fitmunch.com.au with your account email address and the reason for your request. If we approve a refund, it is processed via Stripe and typically appears in your account within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Trial

14-day trial

Paid plans start with a 14-day trial. Stripe collects a card before the trial begins. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. That is how Stripe runs the trial. It is not a 14-day refund after a paid charge. Refunds after a charge are at our discretion. See the refund policy.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you have not cancelled, the subscription continues and Stripe bills the card on file. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged.

Cancel

How do I cancel?

Sign in and open Billing. You can cancel at any time in Billing. Stripe handles it. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. After the first charge, subscriptions auto-renew unless you cancel. Cancel anytime in Billing; access continues to the end of the paid period. If you can't sign in, email support@fitmunch.com.au. The Cancel subscription button on this page opens that same Billing path.

Account

Can my personal trainer see my data?

Only if you connect your account to their PT dashboard via an invite link. You control sharing at all times.

How do I sign in?

Use Sign in. Forgot the password? The sign-in page can email you a reset link.

Data and privacy

See our privacy policy and terms of service. For a copy of your data or a deletion request, email support@fitmunch.com.au.

App

Getting started

FitMunch helps you track nutrition, scan receipts, plan meals, and stay on top of your food goals. New to FitMunch? Start with our homepage or check pricing. FitMunch is a browser web app. On iPhone, Add to Home Screen. An App Store listing is not live yet.

How does receipt scanning work?

Upload a photo of your supermarket receipt in the browser. FitMunch extracts items and matches nutrition data. The photo is processed and not stored on our servers. Saved meals and plans stay with your account.
