Support

Cancel, trial, and receipts

How do I cancel?

Sign in and open Billing. You can cancel at any time in Billing. Stripe handles it. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. After the first charge, subscriptions auto-renew unless you cancel. Cancel anytime in Billing; access continues to the end of the paid period. If you can't sign in, email support@fitmunch.com.au.

14-day trial

Paid plans start with a 14-day trial. Stripe collects a card before the trial begins. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. That is how Stripe runs the trial. It is not a 14-day refund after a paid charge. Refunds after a charge are at our discretion. See the refund policy.

Receipts or invoices

For a receipt or an invoice, see the refund policy or email support@fitmunch.com.au with your account email address and the reason for your request. If we approve a refund, it is processed via Stripe and typically appears in your account within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Email: support@fitmunch.com.au. Response time: 1–2 business days.

Getting started

FitMunch helps you track nutrition, scan receipts, plan meals, and stay on top of your food goals. New to FitMunch? Start with our homepage or check pricing.

Common questions

How does receipt scanning work?

Upload a photo of your supermarket receipt in the browser. FitMunch extracts items and matches nutrition data. The photo is processed and not stored on our servers. Saved meals and plans stay with your account.

Can my personal trainer see my data?

Only if you connect your account to their PT dashboard via an invite link. You control sharing at all times.
