Why a list from your week beats a generic one

Generic protein lists

A list of high protein foods tells you what to buy, not how much. You still have to work out meals and quantities for your own target.

FitMunch list

The quantities come from the meals in your week, so the list matches the protein target you set, not someone else's.

Staples that often show up

Eggs, chicken, tuna, Greek yoghurt, cottage cheese, lentils and tofu are common in a high protein week. Your list depends on your plan, your dietary needs and what you already have at home.

Questions people ask

Where do I shop the list?
Anywhere you like. FitMunch writes a list from your own meal plan using common Australian supermarket foods, and you shop it yourself.
How much protein will the list cover?
The plan is written to the daily protein target in your profile. You can change the target, and the next plan follows it.
Does FitMunch show prices or place my order?
No. The list is ingredients and quantities. FitMunch does not show live supermarket prices. FitMunch never places or pays for your supermarket order.
Can I change the plan before I shop?
Yes. Change your targets or dietary requirements and generate a new week. The list follows the new plan.
