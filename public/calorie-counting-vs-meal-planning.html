How it works

Counting records the past. Planning writes the next 7 days. FitMunch does the planning: meals, training and one shopping list. It does not promise a health result.

01

Counting

A count is a log of meals you already ate. Useful as a record. It does not write next week or a shopping list.

02

Planning

You set calorie and protein targets. FitMunch writes 7 days of meals and training before you shop.

03

Then the list

The shopping list is the food for the meals you lock. You check out at the store yourself.

Questions people ask

Does FitMunch replace a food log?
It plans the week ahead. You still choose what you eat. The plan is meals written to targets you set, plus a shopping list.
Do I have to count every meal by hand?
No. You set the targets and FitMunch writes the week. You can change the targets and write a new week.
Which one shops for me?
Neither. FitMunch never places or pays for your supermarket order. You check out at the store.
Is one of them a medical plan?
No. Both are ways to organise food. FitMunch is food planning support, not medical advice.
