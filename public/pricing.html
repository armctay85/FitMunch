Questions

How does the 14-day trial work?

Paid plans start with a 14-day trial. Stripe collects a card before the trial begins. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. That is how the trial works. It is not a 14-day refund after a paid charge.

Can I cancel anytime?

Cancel anytime in Billing. After the first charge, subscriptions auto-renew unless you cancel. Access continues to the end of the paid period.

How does billing work?

Paid plans are billed through Stripe. Premium and the Coach plans are monthly. A yearly Premium price is shown on this page when it is available. Free starts as a free account with no card.

How do refunds work?

Refunds after a charge are at our discretion. There is no automatic money-back window after the first charge. Email support@fitmunch.com.au with your account email and the reason. We reply within 1 business day. If a refund is approved, it is processed through Stripe and typically appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. See the refund policy.
