Questions people ask

How do I make a weekly meal plan for a family?
Pick the dinners first, repeat ingredients on purpose, then write one shopping list for the whole week. FitMunch writes that list from the meals you commit and drafts the trolley. Check the shelf price at the store.
Does the plan assume a family of 4?
No. Quantities follow the meals you lock, not a fixed family size. A household of 2 and a household of 5 get different lists because the weeks they commit are different.
Can I plan the household shop around my own targets?
Yes. You log your calorie and protein targets and commit the meals the household will eat. FitMunch does not keep a separate plan for each person in the house.
Can I print the family meal plan?
You can print the week and the list straight from the browser, or copy them into your own Woolworths or Coles online order.
Does FitMunch order or pay for the family shop?
No. FitMunch drafts the trolley. You check out at the store yourself and pay Woolworths, Coles or Aldi directly.
