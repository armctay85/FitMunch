Common questions from PTs

Do my clients need to pay for FitMunch?
No. Your clients get free access to the full FitMunch app: macro tracking, receipt scanner, shopping lists, progress sharing. You pay the PT subscription, they get the app for free.
How does the receipt scanner work?
Clients snap a photo of their Woolworths or Coles receipt. FitMunch reads every item and calculates the macro breakdown: protein, carbs, fats, calories. It's the feature that drives daily logging because it turns a 10-minute job into 30 seconds.
What if my clients already use MyFitnessPal?
MyFitnessPal is great for consumers but gives PTs zero visibility. FitMunch is built around the trainer's dashboard, you see what every client logs, in real time, from one place.
How long does it take to onboard my clients?
Under 5 minutes total. Generate an invite link from your dashboard. Clients click it, create an account, and they're immediately in your roster with any plans you've assigned.
Is FitMunch built for Australian supermarkets?
Yes, the receipt scanner is built for Woolworths, Coles and ALDI receipts. The nutrition database covers Australian products.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard at any time. No lock-in contracts, no cancellation fees. Your clients retain access until the end of your billing period.
