How it works

A beginner week is meals and training written to targets you set. FitMunch includes weekly training plans and workout tracking with the meals. It does not promise a fitness result.

01

Start with targets you set

You log a goal and the calorie and protein targets. FitMunch does not promise what training will do.

02

Meals and training

The week holds 7 days of meals plus a training plan. You can change either and write a new week.

03

Shop the food yourself

The shopping list is the food for the meals you lock. You check out at the store.

Questions people ask

Is this a beginner program with promised results?
No. It is a first week of meals and training written to targets you set. FitMunch does not promise a fitness result.
Do I need a trainer first?
No. You can plan your own week. Personal trainers can also draft a week for a client from the trainer tools.
What is in the week?
Meals, a training plan, and one shopping list. You check out at the store yourself.
Is this medical advice?
No. Food and training planning support only, not medical advice.
