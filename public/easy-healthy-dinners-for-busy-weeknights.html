How it works

A busy weeknight is simpler when dinner is already on the plan. FitMunch writes those dinners into the same 7-day plan as the rest of the week, then one shopping list.

01

Pick the dinners

You commit the dinners you will actually eat. Breakfasts and lunches can sit on the same list.

02

Write them to your targets

The week follows the calorie and protein targets you set. FitMunch does not promise a health result.

03

One shop

Shared foods are listed once. You check out at the store. FitMunch never places or pays for the order.

Questions people ask

Are these dinners a health treatment?
No. They are meals in a weekly plan. FitMunch does not promise a health result and this is not medical advice.
Can I repeat a dinner?
Yes. Repeat a protein or a carb on purpose and it becomes one line on the shopping list.
Do I still shop myself?
Yes. Copy or print the list and check out at the store, in person or in your own online order.
What else is in the week?
Meals and training, then the shopping list. FitMunch is a health engine for food, fitness and shopping.
