A list of high protein foods tells you what to buy, not how much. You still have to work out meals and quantities for your own target.

FitMunch list

Questions people ask

Is this an official Woolworths shopping list?
No. FitMunch is a separate web app and is not affiliated with Woolworths. It writes a list from your own meal plan, and you shop it at Woolworths yourself.
How much protein will the list cover?
The plan is written to the daily protein target in your profile. You can change the target, and the next plan follows it.
Does FitMunch show Woolworths prices or place my order?
No. The list is ingredients and quantities. FitMunch does not show live supermarket prices and never places or pays for an order.
Can I change the plan before I shop?
Yes. Change your targets, budget or dietary requirements and generate a new week. The list follows the new plan.
