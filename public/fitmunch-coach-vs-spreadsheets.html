Same job, different tool

Spreadsheet

You type every meal, look up specials yourself, total the ingredients, and email a file. The grid does not know the client targets until you put them there, and it does not draft a supermarket list.

FitMunch Coach

You set the targets. Coach writes the 7 days, drafts one list, and gives you a PT-branded share link or PDF.

What stays in your hands

01

The targets

You still decide the numbers. Coach does not invent a goal. It plans the week from the targets you enter for that client.

02

The store

The client checks out at the store, the same as they would with a spreadsheet.

03

The checkout

Neither tool pays the supermarket. The client checks out at Woolworths, Coles or Aldi and pays the store.

Questions trainers ask

How is FitMunch Coach different from a spreadsheet?
A spreadsheet holds whatever you type. Coach writes the 7-day plan from the client targets, drafts a list, and gives you a branded share link or PDF.
Can I still give the client a PDF?
Yes. Coach can share a link or a PDF. You are not locked to one format.
Do I still check Woolworths, Coles and Aldi myself?
It is not a live trolley and FitMunch does not order or pay.
What does FitMunch Coach cost?
A$39 a month for up to 10 clients, or A$79 a month unlimited, with a 14-day trial. A card is required. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged.
Will a spreadsheet do the supermarket checkout?
No. Neither a spreadsheet nor FitMunch checks out or pays at the supermarket. The client pays the store.
Is Coach a substitute for a dietitian?
No. Clients should see a dietitian for medical nutrition. FitMunch is food planning support, not medical advice.
