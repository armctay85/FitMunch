Questions people ask

Is there a Coles meal planner app?
Coles publishes meal planning ideas and recipe sets on its own site. FitMunch is a separate web app that runs in your browser. It writes a full 7-day plan from your body targets and drafts a Coles trolley. FitMunch is not affiliated with Coles. Check the shelf price at the store.
Does it use the Coles specials this week?
The draft is an ingredient list for the week. It is not a live trolley, so check the shelf price at the store.
Can I plan a month of Coles meals?
FitMunch plans 1 week at a time. Commit each week, then draft the next list from the meals you lock.
Does FitMunch order from Coles for me?
No. FitMunch drafts the trolley. You check out at Coles yourself, in store or through your own Coles account.
