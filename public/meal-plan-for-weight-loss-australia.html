How it works

If the goal you set is weight loss, the plan is still a week of meals written to targets you choose. FitMunch does not guarantee weight loss, muscle gain, or any health outcome.

01

Log where you are heading

You log the weight you are and the weight you are heading for. You set the calorie and protein targets.

02

Write the meals

FitMunch plans 7 days of meals and training to those targets. This is food planning, not a promised result.

03

Shop it yourself

One shopping list follows the meals you lock. You check out at the store. FitMunch never places or pays for the order.
