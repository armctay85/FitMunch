What a trainer gets

The trainer gets a meal plan for that client, a draft supermarket list, and a share link or PDF.

FitMunch Coach is the plan builder for a personal trainer. You work from your client roster, not a blank spreadsheet.

  • A 7-day meal plan from the calorie, protein, carb, and fat targets you set for that client.
  • Targets filled from the client's meal logs when they have logged in the last 7 days.
  • Dietary flags for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, and fish-free.
  • A draft supermarket list scaled to household size.
  • Your practice name, accent colour, and logo on the share page and the PDF.
  • A share link you send, plus a PDF the client can download.
  • Adherence from the last 7 days of meal logs: days logged and average macros against the plan.

How Coach works

  1. Pick a client.
  2. Set calorie, protein, carb and fat targets, or fill them from that client's meal logs.
  3. Choose dietary flags and household size, then generate the 7-day plan and the draft supermarket list.
  4. Add your practice name, accent colour and logo, then send a share link or a PDF.
