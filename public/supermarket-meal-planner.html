Supermarket meal plans vs a week written for you

Retailer meal plans

Supermarkets publish weeknight dinner plans with a shopping list. They are written for a family table, usually 5 dinners, with no link to your own targets.

FitMunch week

All 7 days, every meal, sized to the body you log. Staples repeat across meals so the same foods cover more of the week, and the list is drafted before you leave home.

Keeping the week simple at the supermarket

Questions people ask

Is there a supermarket meal planner app?
Supermarkets publish meal plans and recipes with shopping lists on their own websites. FitMunch is a separate web app that runs in your browser. It writes a full 7-day plan from your body targets and drafts a supermarket trolley. FitMunch is not affiliated with any supermarket.
Is there an AI meal planner for supermarket shopping?
FitMunch plans the week from the targets you log, then drafts the trolley. It is not a live trolley feed.
Can I get my supermarket meal plan as a PDF?
You can copy the list or print it straight from the browser, then take it into the store or type it into your own supermarket online order.
How do I plan a week of meals for 1, 2 or 4?
Pick meals that share staples, cook in batches and repeat lunches. FitMunch writes the ingredient list from the meals you commit, so the quantities follow the week you lock rather than a fixed family size.
Does FitMunch publish supermarket prices?
No. The draft is an ingredient list. You check out at the store.
Does FitMunch order or pay for my supermarket shop?
No. FitMunch drafts the trolley. You check out at the supermarket yourself and pay the store directly.
