How it works

The shop stays closer to what you will eat when the list is built from the meals you already committed. FitMunch writes that list. It does not show supermarket prices and it does not quote a figure for the shop.

01

Commit the meals

Pick the week you will actually eat. Food you did not plan is not added to the list.

02

One list from that week

FitMunch turns the locked meals into one shopping list. Repeated foods become one line.

03

You check out

You shop the list yourself. FitMunch never places or pays for the order and does not show supermarket prices.

Questions people ask

Does this promise I will spend a certain way?
No. FitMunch does not show supermarket prices and does not quote the shop. It writes the list from the meals you commit.
How does that relate to wasted food?
The list is the food for the meals you locked, so items outside that week are not on it.
Can I still shop in person?
Yes. Copy or print the list and check out at the store, or type it into your own online order.
Who pays?
You pay the store. FitMunch never places or pays for your supermarket order.
