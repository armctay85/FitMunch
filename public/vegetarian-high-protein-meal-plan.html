How it works

Pick a vegetarian preference and FitMunch plans the week from foods that fit, such as tofu, tempeh, lentils and chickpeas. The protein target is one you set.

01

Choose vegetarian

Pick your dietary requirements. The week is planned from staples that fit them.

02

Set the protein target

03

Questions people ask

Can the week be vegetarian?
Yes. Pick a vegetarian preference and the week is planned from foods that fit, such as tofu, tempeh, lentils and chickpeas.
Who sets the protein target?
You do. FitMunch does not choose how much protein you need. It plans to the target you enter.
Does it order the shop?
No. FitMunch does not show supermarket prices and never places or pays for your supermarket order.
Can I add other requirements?
Yes. Dietary requirements such as gluten free can be set with the vegetarian preference, and the week follows them.
