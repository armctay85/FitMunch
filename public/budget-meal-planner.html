How to shop the method

  1. Set a weekly protein target. Bodyweight in kg times 1.6 to 2.2g, spread across 7 days.
  2. Rank the shelf by $/25g protein, not the sticker price you see first.
  3. Anchor the trolley with 3 to 4 of the cheapest proteins from the list above before anything else goes in.
  4. Add one carb base (rice, oats, wholemeal wraps) and one bag of frozen veg to round out volume.
  5. Scan the receipt when you get home. FitMunch confirms the macros and drafts the gaps for next week's shop.
