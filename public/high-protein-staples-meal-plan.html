Why plan around staples

A new recipe every night

Seven different recipes mean seven sets of ingredients, and it is hard to know whether the week adds up to your protein target.

A staples week

A handful of protein staples repeat across the week in different meals, so the list stays short and every day is written to your targets.

Questions people ask

What counts as a staple?
Foods you can find in most Australian supermarkets every week, such as eggs, chicken, tuna, Greek yoghurt, lentils, tofu and oats. FitMunch plans the week from foods like these.
Will the plan hit my protein target?
The week is written to the daily calorie and protein targets in your profile, and each day is checked against the calorie target before you see it. You can change the targets and generate a new week.
Can I plan vegetarian, vegan or gluten free?
Yes. Pick your dietary requirements and the week is planned from staples that fit them, such as tofu, tempeh, lentils and chickpeas for a plant-based week.
Does FitMunch show prices or place my order?
No. The plan and list are meals, ingredients and quantities. FitMunch does not show supermarket prices and never places or pays for your supermarket order.
