Questions people ask

Is there a supermarket meal planner app?
Supermarkets publish meal planning ideas and recipe sets on their own websites. FitMunch is a separate web app that runs in your browser. It writes a full 7-day plan from your body targets and drafts a supermarket trolley. FitMunch is not affiliated with any supermarket.
Does it show supermarket prices?
The draft is an ingredient list for the week. It is not a live trolley.
Can I plan a month of supermarket meals?
FitMunch plans 1 week at a time. Commit each week, then draft the next list from the meals you lock.
Does FitMunch order from the supermarket for me?
No. FitMunch drafts the trolley. You check out at the supermarket yourself, in store or through your own supermarket account.
