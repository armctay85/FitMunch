Questions trainers ask

Can a personal trainer build client meal plans for Woolworths?
Yes. FitMunch Coach writes 7 days from the client targets you set and drafts a Woolworths list priced from public catalogue specials.
Where do the Woolworths draft prices come from?
The draft is priced from public catalogue specials. Check the shelf price at checkout.
Can the same plan use Coles or Aldi?
Yes. Coach can draft the list from public Coles or Aldi specials as well. FitMunch does not place the order at any of them.
How does the client receive the Woolworths plan?
You send a PT-branded share link or a PDF. The client shops the list themselves.
Does FitMunch pay Woolworths for the shop?
No. FitMunch drafts the list. The client checks out at Woolworths and pays Woolworths directly.
Who should not use this as medical nutrition?
Anyone who needs medical nutrition advice. Clients should see a dietitian for medical nutrition. FitMunch is food planning support only.
