Questions people ask

What is a macro meal planner?
A way to write the week against the calorie and protein targets you set, instead of picking recipes and hoping the numbers land. FitMunch writes the 7 days from the targets you log.
Does FitMunch choose my calorie and protein targets?
No. You log the calorie and protein targets. FitMunch plans to those numbers. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace advice from a dietitian.
Is this the same as a budget high protein week?
No. The budget meal planner ranks staples by protein per dollar. This page writes the week to the targets you set. The draft is still priced from public catalogue specials.
Can I turn the macro week into a shopping list?
Yes. The meals become one ingredient list, priced from public catalogue specials. Print it from the browser or copy it into your own online order.
Does FitMunch order or pay for my shop?
No. FitMunch drafts the trolley. You check out at the store yourself and pay Woolworths, Coles or Aldi directly.
