Questions people ask

How do I meal plan for one person?
Choose the week first, repeat a few meals on purpose, and buy what those meals use. FitMunch writes the plan from your targets and the list from the meals you commit.
Will the trolley be family sized?
No. Quantities follow the week you lock. The worked example on this page is for one adult. Your list is written from your own week.
What if I want leftovers for lunch?
Commit the lunch as its own meal. The ingredient line includes it once, so the next lunch is planned rather than left to chance.
Is a meal plan for one only dinners?
No. The week covers breakfast, lunch, dinner and a snack, so you are not writing a second list midweek.
Does FitMunch order or pay for my shop?
No. FitMunch drafts the trolley. You check out at the store yourself and pay Woolworths, Coles or Aldi directly.
