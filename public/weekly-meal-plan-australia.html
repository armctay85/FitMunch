How it works

FitMunch is a health engine for food, fitness and shopping. The week is meals, training and one list. You shop that list yourself.

01

Log a goal

You log your body and a goal. Calories and protein for the day come from the targets you set.

02

Plan seven days

FitMunch writes 7 days of meals and training. You can change the targets and write a new week.

03

Shop the list

The shopping list is the food for the meals you lock. You check out at the store. FitMunch never places or pays for the order.

Questions people ask

What is a weekly meal plan here?
Seven days of meals and training written to calorie and protein targets you set, then one shopping list for that week.
Does FitMunch order the shop?
No. FitMunch drafts the list. You check out at the store and pay the store directly. FitMunch never places or pays for your supermarket order.
Is training part of the same week?
Yes. The health engine plans meals and training together, then writes the shopping list from the meals you lock.
Where does the trial run?
The trial is on the web. Card on file. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged.
