Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Policy version 2026-10.

1. What we collect

Account data: email address, name (if provided), and account preferences when you sign up.

Receipt data: when you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to our AI provider to read the item names, quantities, pack sizes, prices, store name and receipt date. FitMunch doesn't keep the photo after the scan. We never store card numbers, loyalty card numbers or the store's address. If you turn on price memory (section 4), the item prices from that scan are saved to your account. If you don't, they aren't. FitMunch does not keep the original receipt photo.

Food and nutrition data: meals you log, foods you add to your pantry, shopping lists you create, meal plans you build, and nutrition preferences you set.

Usage data: pages visited, features used, and anonymised interaction data to improve FitMunch.

PT/client relationship data: if you connect with a personal trainer through FitMunch, your trainer may view shared nutrition and meal plan data you've opted into sharing.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide the FitMunch service: receipt scanning, meal planning, nutrition tracking, shopping lists, pantry management.
  • If you turn on price memory, to show you your own past prices.
  • To improve our AI extraction and food matching accuracy. Price memory data is not used for this.
  • To communicate service updates, billing, and support responses.
  • To personalise your experience: meal suggestions, nutrition insights, and food recommendations.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. AI and automated processing

FitMunch uses AI to extract items from receipt images and match them to nutrition data. The photo is sent to our AI provider for that scan, then discarded on FitMunch servers. Extracted text and macros may be stored if you save them to your account. Price memory, if you turn it on, stores the grocery lines described in section 4. It does not store the photo.

4. Price memory (optional)

Price memory is off unless you turn it on. It is free. It is not part of Premium, the trial, or sign-up.

What we save: for each grocery line on a receipt you scan, the item name as printed, the price you paid, quantity and pack size, the store name (for example Coles) and the date on the receipt. We leave out pharmacy, medicine, alcohol, tobacco and gift-card lines.

Why: to show you what you paid before, next to items on your shopping list. That's the only use. The prices shown are your past prices, not the store's current price. Always check the price at checkout.

Who can see it: Only you. Not your personal trainer, other FitMunch users, supermarkets or advertisers. We don't sell it, and we don't combine it with other people's prices.

How long: up to 18 months from the receipt date, then it is deleted automatically. You can delete one receipt, or all of it, at any time in Settings > Price memory. Deleting your account deletes all of it.

Turning it off: stops saving straight away. We'll ask whether you also want to delete what's already saved. The default is to delete it.

Getting a copy: Settings > Price memory > Download my prices, or email us.

If we ever want to use combined, de-identified prices from many users, we will ask you separately first. Turning on price memory is not agreement to that.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit. We use industry-standard security practices and regularly review our systems. Price memory rows are limited to your account.

6. Data sharing

We share data only:

  • With your personal trainer, if you've connected your account and opted into sharing. Price memory is never shared with your personal trainer.
  • With our service providers (hosting, AI processing, payment processing via Stripe) under data processing agreements.
  • When required by law or to protect our rights.

7. Overseas disclosure (APP 8)

Our AI provider (Google, for receipt reading) may process receipt photos outside Australia, including in the United States. Our app servers run in Sydney. The photo is used to read that scan and is not kept by FitMunch afterwards.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • Access your data by emailing support@fitmunch.com.au.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Correct an item match or delete any saved price in Settings.
  • Opt out of AI-based receipt processing at any time.
  • Export your meal plans, food logs, pantry data, and price memory.
  • Withdraw PT sharing at any time from your account settings.

If you're not happy with how we handled a privacy issue, email us. If we don't resolve it, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

10. Children

FitMunch is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We'll notify you of material changes to this policy via email or in-app notice before they take effect. A material change to price memory bumps the policy version and asks you again.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: support@fitmunch.com.au

Data deletion requests: support@fitmunch.com.au

FitMunch provides general food planning support. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
