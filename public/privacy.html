Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What we collect

Account data: email address, name (if provided), and account preferences when you sign up.

Receipt data: when you scan a receipt, the photo is sent to Google Gemini, our AI receipt reader, to extract item names, quantities, and prices. Extracted item and nutrition data may stay with your account if you save a meal, plan, or log.

Food and nutrition data: meals you log, foods you add to your pantry, shopping lists you create, meal plans you build, and nutrition preferences you set.

Usage data: this is not anonymised. It sits on your account. We keep your name, your email, and your user ID. Purchase history is tied to that same user ID: the app signs you into RevenueCat with your account id. We count how many AI features you use each month so the free cap can apply. Meals and macros you log stay on the account. A receipt photo you upload is that account's scan. FitMunch does not keep the original receipt photo on our servers after the scan response. None of this is used for tracking or ads.

PT/client relationship data: if you connect with a personal trainer through FitMunch, your trainer may view shared nutrition and meal plan data you've opted into sharing.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide the FitMunch service: receipt scanning, meal planning, nutrition tracking, shopping lists, pantry management.
  • To improve our AI extraction and food matching accuracy.
  • To communicate service updates, billing, and support responses.
  • To personalise your experience: meal suggestions, nutrition insights, and food recommendations.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. AI and automated processing

Google Gemini is the AI receipt reader. It reads the receipt photo you upload, tied to your account, and returns item names for nutrition matching. That processing happens overseas.

4. Data storage and security

FitMunch is hosted on Vercel. Account data is stored there and with our database provider, and it is processed overseas. We encrypt data in transit and review access to the account.

5. Data sharing

We share data only:

  • With your personal trainer, if you've connected your account and opted into sharing.
  • With service providers who run FitMunch for us: Vercel for hosting, Stripe for card payments on the web, RevenueCat for App Store purchases (signed in with your account id), and Google Gemini, the AI receipt reader that reads a photo you upload. These providers process data overseas.
  • When required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Your rights

You can:

  • Access your data by emailing support@fitmunch.com.au.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Withdraw PT sharing at any time from your account settings.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Children

FitMunch is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We'll notify you of material changes to this policy via email or in-app notice before they take effect.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: support@fitmunch.com.au

Data deletion requests: support@fitmunch.com.au

FitMunch provides general food planning support. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
