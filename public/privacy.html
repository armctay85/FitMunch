Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What we collect

Everything below is linked to your FitMunch account, so it is personal information. We don't use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites, we don't sell it, and we don't use advertising or tracking tools.

Website-only data: shopping lists, pantry items and meal plans you create on fitmunch.com.au are saved to your account. The public website also records page views and button clicks with FitMunch's own script. Each event carries a session id stored in the browser for that visit, plus the path of the page. It is not tied to your account or your email, and it is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not load a third-party analytics script.

Trainer sharing: if you connect with a personal trainer through FitMunch, your trainer can see the nutrition and meal plan data you choose to share.

What we don't collect: location, contacts, your device's advertising ID, Apple Health or HealthKit data, or your card number (Apple or Stripe (United States) take payment details). In the iOS app, workout logs stay on your phone.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide the FitMunch service: receipt scanning, meal planning, nutrition tracking, shopping lists, pantry management.
  • For subscription analytics: RevenueCat (United States) customer history and charts, tied to your user ID. Website card payments go through Stripe (United States).
  • To improve our AI extraction and food matching accuracy.
  • To communicate service updates, billing, and support responses.
  • To personalise your experience: meal suggestions, nutrition insights, and food recommendations.

None of this is used for ads or tracking. We do not sell your personal data.

3. AI and automated processing

When you scan a receipt while signed in, the photo is tied to your account and sent to an AI provider so it can read the items. We don't keep the original photo on our servers after the scan. Extracted meals and macros stay on the account if you save them.

The app can call Google Gemini, xAI (Grok, United States), OpenAI (United States), and Anthropic (United States). Which one runs depends on which key is configured. Google Gemini is the AI receipt reader when a Gemini key is set.

Google's Gemini API terms say prompts and responses may be stored or cached in any country where Google or its agents maintain facilities, and those terms do not name one country for the Gemini API this app calls. Where a country is not set in our config or named in the provider's docs, data may be processed outside Australia, including the United States.

4. Data storage and security

FitMunch is hosted on Vercel. The deploy config sets the region to Sydney (syd1), in Australia. Account data is stored with Neon (database hosting, Amazon Web Services us-east-1, United States). We encrypt data in transit and review access to the account.

5. Data sharing

We share data only:

  • With your personal trainer, if you've connected your account and opted into sharing.
  • With service providers who run FitMunch for us: Vercel, which hosts the app, Neon, which hosts the database, Stripe (United States) for card payments on the website, RevenueCat (United States) for App Store purchases, Resend (United States) for welcome and password reset emails, Google Gemini, xAI (Grok, United States), OpenAI (United States), and Anthropic (United States).
  • When required by law or to protect our rights.

Australian Privacy Principle 8 (APP 8) applies when personal information is disclosed to an overseas recipient.

Vercel runs this app in Sydney, Australia. Neon hosts the database in the United States. Welcome emails and password reset emails go through Resend (United States). Website card payments go through Stripe (United States). App Store purchases go through RevenueCat (United States).

Google's Gemini API terms say prompts and responses may be stored or cached in any country where Google or its agents maintain facilities, and those terms do not name one country for the Gemini API this app calls. Where a country is not set in our config or named in the provider's docs, data may be processed outside Australia, including the United States.

The same kind of request can go to xAI (Grok, United States), OpenAI (United States), and Anthropic (United States) when those providers are configured.

6. Your rights

You can:

  • Access your data by emailing support@fitmunch.com.au.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Withdraw PT sharing at any time from your account settings.

Complaints: contact support@fitmunch.com.au first. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Children

FitMunch is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We'll notify you of material changes to this policy via email or in-app notice before they take effect.
