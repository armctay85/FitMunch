How it works

If the goal you set is muscle gain, FitMunch writes meals and training to the calorie and protein targets you choose. It does not guarantee muscle gain or any health outcome.

01

Set the targets

You choose the calorie and protein targets for the goal you logged. FitMunch does not invent a result.

02

Plan meals and training

The health engine writes 7 days of meals and training to those targets.

03

One list, your shop

The shopping list is the food for the meals you lock. You check out at the store yourself.

Questions people ask

Does FitMunch guarantee muscle gain?
No. FitMunch does not guarantee muscle gain or any health outcome. It plans meals to targets you set.
Where does the protein number come from?
From you. You set the protein target. FitMunch writes the week to that target and does not tell you the number to use.
Is training included?
Yes. Weekly training plans sit with the meals. The shopping list still comes from the meals you lock.
Who pays for the shop?
You do. FitMunch never places or pays for your supermarket order.
